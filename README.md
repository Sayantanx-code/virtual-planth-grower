# 🌱 Virtual Plant Grower

A fun, interactive frontend project where users grow virtual plants over time!  
This project simulates plant growth based on user interactions and time, with support for animations, sound effects, and Firebase login.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Plant Growth Tracking** – Watch your plant evolve from a seed to a bloom as you water it.
- **Rain Animation** – Enjoy a rain effect whenever you water your plant.
- **Sound Effects** – Get satisfying water sounds with every interaction.
- **Growth Timer** – Your plant progresses even when you're away.
- **Firebase Authentication** – Sign up and log in to save your progress.
- **Multiple Plants** – Grow and switch between different plants.

---

## 🚀 Technologies Used

- **HTML, CSS, JavaScript**
- **Firebase Authentication**
- **LocalStorage (with option to sync to Firebase Database)**
- **CSS Animations & Audio API**
